Ranking Methodology

CoinOsprey rankings are calculated using a combination of market capitalization, trading volume and price-performance data sourced from major exchanges and market data providers. Market capitalization reflects circulating supply multiplied by current price, while trading volume measures liquidity and market activity over a rolling 24-hour window.

Crypto markets move quickly and data changes frequently — prices, rankings and volumes shown here are refreshed on a regular cycle and may differ slightly from real-time exchange data at the moment of viewing. Rankings are provided for informational purposes only and should not be treated as financial advice.
