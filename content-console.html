Advertising Guidelines
Home › Advertising › Guidelines · Admin reference for restricted, deceptive or review-required advertising.
Review-required / potentially restricted
Misleading financial claimsClaims that omit material conditions, risks or important limitations.
Guaranteed returnsPromises or guarantees of profit, yield, capital protection or risk-free gains.
Fraudulent projectsKnown scams, fake token offerings or promotions using fabricated credentials.
PhishingLinks or creatives designed to steal passwords, wallet credentials, seed phrases or payment data.
MalwareMalicious downloads, scripts, exploit payloads or creatives that facilitate harmful software.
ImpersonationAds pretending to be CoinOsprey, exchanges, projects, regulators, public figures or other organizations.
Illegal servicesPromotions for services or transactions that violate applicable law or platform rules.
Deceptive promotionsFake urgency, hidden terms, fabricated scarcity, fake endorsements or materially misleading offers.
Unverified investment claimsInvestment claims without adequate substantiation, source disclosure or required risk language.
Admin approval remains mandatory where configured.
Do not activate or publish an advertisement merely because its creative and URL pass technical validation. Escalate questionable financial, legal, identity or fraud-related claims for human review.
